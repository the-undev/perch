Snooze round

Three looks the spec leaves open: the z on a sleeping bird, the dimmed snoozed bird, and the snooze control in the bubble and on the bird's page. Drawn as the app draws them.

Each section ends with the choice made, for you to agree or change.

The z

An asleep bird has closed eyes and a z in its own colour, where the song note sits. Each bird is shown awake, then asleep.

Choice. A lower-case italic z in the display font, in the bird's breast colour (green, amber, and the fence's dim grey for a fence bird), at the note's position and size. It fades in and out as the note does, and holds still with reduced motion. Closed eyes are a short downward curve in place of the dot. The fence bird's z is dim because the bird is: it reads as quiet, not missing.

The snoozed bird

A snoozed bird beside a fence bird, by day and by night, at three plumpnesses (outstanding work 0, 25 and 50).

Choice. The snoozed bird wears exactly the fence's dimming: dim plumage and dim breast, whatever its mood. By day it is told from the fence bird by closed eyes and the z. By night the two look the same, as the spec accepts; only the wire it stands on (the bright wire, against the fence's thick dark rail) tells them apart. A snoozed bird keeps its plumpness and does not bob.

The bubble

An adopted bird's bubble gets a snooze key beside the launch actions. Pressing it opens the six durations. A snoozed bird's bubble shows the state word, the end and a wake key in its place.

Choice. The snooze key is an icon with the word "Snooze", after the launch icons and before the full-story key, so the row reads actions, then snooze, then the way out. Its durations open beneath it as a small dark card, shortest first, with indefinitely set apart under a rule. The wake key takes the same place, with a sun in place of the moon. The end sits on its own line under the name, in quiet paper ink, worded "until Friday" within six days and "until 4 December" beyond; "until you wake it" when there is no end. The speech carries the waking prefix, as for any asleep bird. The state word is quiet grey, as for the fence; the chips keep their true colours.
