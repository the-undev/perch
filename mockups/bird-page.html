Decisions for this round

  1. The perch. The bird leaves the wire for a perch, a T-stand at the page's top right, full size, idle animations running, always facing left toward its own record. Its bubble stays attached and continues word for word, but drops its name-and-state header here, because the page's heading already says both: the bubble is pure speech and chips. One move fills the empty corner, brings the bird in, carries the bubble on, and makes the app's name literal. Recommended.
  2. Chips as handles. A bubble chip on this page presses through to the ledger row that holds its working, marked with the same dot; the ledger grows a spelled-out line per source (each forge's newest CI run, named and linked). Recommended over tooltips: the working earns a place on the page, not a hover.
  3. The commit river. One list for every commit, three marks for where it has reached, hollow when a remote lacks it, one amber sentence when remotes diverge. Replaces the per-forge lists that doubled the page's height. Recommended.
  4. The papers rise. Opening the papers slides the sheet up over the written record, which dims; the bird stays on its post through the signing. The papers become a view of their own without leaving the place. Recommended over a separate route or full-page swap.
  5. The camera pans. Between birds the strip holds still and the ground pans toward the next bird's place on the wire, the flyer crossing above; the swap flash goes. Panning direction comes from the birds' order, which the strip already knows. Recommended.
