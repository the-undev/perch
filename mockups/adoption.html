Adoption papers

Adopting a bird today is one press that hides every choice: the wire, the name, the tier and the expectations only appear afterwards, inside an editor you have to know to open. This round makes adoption a moment with a form of its own, the way a real aviary would hand you papers.

1The shape of it

No floating dialog. perch draws a world, and the world already has one warm material: paper, laid on the ground of a bird's page. The adoption form is a sheet of adoption papers that unfolds there, and the same sheet reopens later to amend, so there is no second, hidden editor to discover.

4Decisions wanting a verdict

1 · Papers on the page, not a dialog

A floating modal is the web's reflex and sits outside perch's world. The papers unfold in the ground area of the bird's page, where the entry form already lives; the bubble's adopt key goes there and unfolds them.

in-world papers. A modal saves nothing and breaks the material language.

2 · The wire picker is drawn as wires

The round's one flourish: the wires as strokes on the paper, the bird sitting on the chosen one. The alternative is a plain radio row like the tiers.

drawn wires. The choice it depicts is the one that confused the fence; seeing the bird land on the wire is the explanation.

3 · What sits above the fold

Unfolded: wire, name, tier, expectations. Folded behind "More particulars", which names its contents even when closed: thresholds, key files, roadmaps, zellij session. Everything is one save either way.

as drawn. The four common choices cover almost every adoption; the fold cures the discoverability failure without crowding the sheet.

4 · One vocabulary: the papers

"Edit the entry" goes. An adopted bird's page offers the papers; amending and adopting are the same sheet with a different signature line. The config vocabulary (entries, keys) stays in the config file and the spec.

rename it. The user should never need to learn that an adoption made an "entry".

5 · Where adopt is offered

Today adoption lives on the bird's page. The fence bird's bubble gets adopt as its one key beside the launches, going to the page with the papers unfolded.

both, one destination. The fence bubble saying only "adopt" is the aviary telling you what a fence bird is for.
