Work sources

Roadmap files and forge tickets, each parsed into bugs and tasks with a priority, shown as a sheet of their own on the paper, and judged one source at a time for the bird.

Bottom up, no config

Bullets and checkboxes are tasks. A bare heading is one task with its prose as the body. Higher headings group.

Two axes from words

Kind is bug or task, priority normal or critical. Whole words in headings, titles, tags and labels, inherited downwards.

Only bugs move the bird

Amber at 5 and red at 20 open bugs; amber at 1 and red at 3 critical. Per source, worst level wins.

The detail page

The paper gains a tab per work source. A roadmap opens parsed, with the markdown a switch away. Tickets get a sheet of their own. The ledger gives each source its own bug rows, with the thresholds as workings.

One tab per source

Documents, then roadmaps, then a ticket tab per forge. The ticket tab carries its open count. A forge with no open tickets still gets its tab, so an empty list reads as none, not unread.

Parsed first

A roadmap opens parsed. Show markdown swaps the sheet to the file as written, as today.

The margin marks

One red bar is a bug, two a critical bug. A task has no mark. A critical task names its priority in red ink on the right, since it carries no bar.

The ledger

A bug row per source, then a critical row only when there are any. The thresholds sit in the workings. Open tasks stay one row, for plumpness.

Ultrawide

The page is capped at 1920px and centred. At 3440px that leaves wide margins. The question below asks what a full width should hold.

To settle

Each needs a decision before implementation.

Does a critical heading make its tasks bugs?

Under the two axes, "Data loss" under Critical is a critical task and leaves the bird green.

Recommendation: no change. Add "loss" and similar to the bug list only if real files need it.

The word lists

Bug: bug, bugs, issue, issues, problem, problems, broken, regression, crash, crashes. Critical: critical, urgent, blocker.

Recommendation: start with these and adjust as files show misfires.

Done items on the sheet

Checked tasks show struck through. A file that keeps months of checked items would bury the open ones.

Recommendation: show them under groups with progress, since the progress needs them.

A full-width layout at 3440px

Options: keep the 1920px cap; or let the paper pane take the width and show two sheets side by side, such as the roadmap and the ticket list.
