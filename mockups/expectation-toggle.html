Decisions for this round

  1. The idiom. C, delete as applicable: both states printed, the pen strikes the one that does not apply. It is the only option where off is a visible statement rather than an absence, which is the exact failure that prompted the round. Recommended. B is the runner-up if C reads as too wide once a second expectation (tickets) returns.
  2. The ink. The strike and the tick are the owner's pen, so they use the signature's ink (paper-strong), never red; red stays reserved for the papers' refusals.
  3. The motion. The mark draws itself in about 150ms when pressed, answering the press; reduced motion gets the finished mark at once.
  4. The accessibility. Whatever the drawing, the control stays one real checkbox whose accessible name is "ci · a pipeline on its forge"; the words and strokes are presentation.
