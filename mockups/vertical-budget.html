The vertical budget

On the fixed stage every pixel above the panes is rent paid for ever. Today the strip and the band spend about 420px before the record starts. This round claws back around 150px by moving words beside things instead of above them: the wire's name onto the wire, the bubble into the band's empty middle, the actions beside the name. Nothing is lost, only the gaps between.

2 · The tightened top

3 · What holds it honest

The bird does not shrink: the stand and the perched bird stay the bird-page round's size, and the band's height is theirs. The bubble keeps a reading width (it grows to about 56ch, no wider), so a long speech still wraps rather than becoming one unreadable line. On a narrow window the band stacks exactly as it does today: heading, then bubble over bird, the strip's label staying on the wire. The strip's label yields if a tab is crowded: the birds centre in the width right of it, and they already bunch by the strip's spacing rule.
